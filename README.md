# Biofall

> **Containment has failed. The city is collapsing.**  
> A fast-paced top-down shooter where a lone operative fights through a quarantined metropolis overrun by bio-organic horrors.

---

## Overview

**Genre:** Top-Down Action Shooter  
**Players:** 1 (Single-player) *(co-op as stretch goal)*  
**Engine:** Unity (URP)  
**Platforms:** PC  

A classified bioengineering experiment has triggered a catastrophic outbreak in a sealed city. The infection mutates organic matter into aggressive, rapidly evolving entities.

You are part of a last-response unit sent into the quarantine zone — not to save the city, but to **contain what’s left of it**.

---

## Key Features

- **High-Density Combat** — Fight overwhelming waves of enemies in tight arenas  
- **Satisfying Gunplay** — Punchy weapons, strong hit feedback, responsive controls  
- **Level-Based Progression** — Structured missions with objectives and extraction  
- **Light Narrative Layer** — Briefings, radio transmissions, environmental storytelling  
- **Dynamic Event System** — Blackouts, surges, lockdowns that alter gameplay  
- **Replayability** — Enemy variation, scaling difficulty, upgrade paths  

---

## Enemy Faction: The Infected

| Enemy | Role | Behavior |
|------|------|----------|
| Walker | Basic Melee | Slow-moving, attacks in groups |
| Runner | Fast Unit | High speed, low HP |
| Brute | Heavy | High durability, resistant to knockback |
| Spitter | Ranged | Fires bio-projectiles |
| Crawler | Control | Roots or slows the player |
| Bloater | Explosive | Detonates on death |
| Leech Cluster | Swarm | Small, fast, overwhelming |

---

## Gameplay Loop

Deploy → Explore Level → Engage Enemies → Complete Objectives → Extract → Upgrade → Next Mission

---

## Objectives

- Survive waves  
- Reach extraction point  
- Activate systems (generators, scanners)  
- Seal contamination zones  
- Eliminate priority targets  

---

## Progression

**Currency:** Bio Samples / Credits  

**Unlocks:**
- Weapons (pistol → rifle → heavy)
- Upgrades:
  - Damage  
  - Fire rate  
  - Max health  
  - Mobility  

Optional perk system for passive bonuses.

---

## Narrative Design

Minimalist, gameplay-first storytelling.

**Delivery:**
- Mission briefings  
- Radio transmissions  
- Environmental clues  

**Story Arc:**
- Early: containment attempt  
- Mid: system collapse  
- Late: origin discovery  

---

## MVP Scope

- 1 level (urban quarantine zone)  
- 1 player character  
- 3–4 weapons  
- 4 enemy types + 1 mini-boss  
- Basic wave system  
- Simple objective (survive & extract)  
- Core UI (HP, ammo, wave)
